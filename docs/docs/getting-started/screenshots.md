# Screenshots

The Posta dashboard manages templates, sending, forms, live chat, subscribers, domains and keys,
in light or dark mode. The forms and the chat widget run on your own website.

## Dashboard

![The Posta dashboard: total emails, delivery rate, unread form messages, send volume and recent emails](/img/screenshots/dashboard.png)

## Analytics

Delivery, bounces and time to deliver for any period, split by mailbox provider.

![Email analytics over 30 days: delivery rate, bounce rate, time to deliver and daily delivered and failed emails](/img/screenshots/analytics.png)

## Templates

Versioned templates in many languages, edited in a visual builder or in code with a live preview.

![A template with its versions and languages](/img/screenshots/template-detail.png)

![The template editor: HTML on the left, the rendered email on the right](/img/screenshots/template-editor.png)

## Forms

### Contact form widget

One script tag puts a styled, accessible form on your website.

![A contact form widget embedded on a website](/img/screenshots/form-widget.png)

### Messages inbox

Contact messages land in a shared inbox, with states, assignees, priorities, tags and spam scoring.

![The Messages inbox with contact form messages, their priority, tags and state](/img/screenshots/messages.png)

## Surveys and signups

### Shared form link

A survey, registration or signup form opened from its share link, with no website needed.

![A survey opened from its share link](/img/screenshots/form-share.png)

### Responses

Answers are kept as responses, summarised question by question, and exported as CSV or JSON.

![Customer feedback responses summarised: average satisfaction and how likely people are to recommend](/img/screenshots/form-responses.png)

## Live chat

### Chat widget

A home screen, help links and live chat on your website, added with one script tag. The home screen
is part of Posta Enterprise.

![The chat widget's home screen open on a website, with a logo, the online agents, a message item and a card](/img/screenshots/chat-widget.png)
